# Publishly brand system

The product-owner-selected name is **Publishly** (including the final `ly`).
Brand strings are centralized through `NEXT_PUBLIC_BRAND_NAME`; changing the
commercial name does not require changing workspace, publishing, or provider
code.

## Position

Publishly is the reliability layer for social posting at scale. Every post ends
with a clear outcome: confirmed live, retrying for a known reason, or waiting for
the operator to fix an account connection. Nothing fails silently.

The primary audience is operators managing authorized accounts across many
brands, clients, locations, or markets. The secondary audience is developers
adding reliable social posting to their own software.

## Voice

- Direct, calm, and operator-to-operator.
- Lead with the outcome, then show the proof.
- Prefer “clear failure reason” to internal engineering terms.
- Frame account scale as multi-brand, multi-client, multi-location, or
  multi-market. Never use bot, farm, or mass-account language.
- Do not claim platform partnership, approval, customer counts, or reliability
  rates without public evidence.

## Visual identity

- Direction: **Clean Sky** — pale blue open space, dark ink, proof cards, status
  dots, halftone texture, and restrained yellow highlights.
- Mark: a `P` whose bowl is a posting window; the blue dot represents a post
  reaching its destination.
- Wordmark: Publishly, sentence case.
- Display and body type: Geist Sans. Technical proof: Geist Mono.
- Core colors: sky paper `#F7FBFF`, ink `#071521`, clear blue `#1878C4`, bright
  sky `#55B0FF`, success `#24A36A`, and signal yellow `#FFD34D`.
- Corners are compact and practical. Motion should explain post state, not act
  as decoration.
- Assets: `publishly.svg`, `publishly-app-icon.svg`, raster app icons, favicon,
  wordmark, and the 1200×630 reliability social preview.

## Naming and legal caution

An initial obvious-conflict screen found existing exact-name uses, including a
social-media publishing product indexed at `vistaar.online`, an App Store
content-publishing app, and `publishly.nl`. This is not a legal trademark
opinion. Obtain counsel-led trademark and domain clearance before a large launch
or platform-review filing under this name.

Do not imply endorsement by Postiz or status as an official partner of any
social platform. Source and license disclosures remain separate from the
customer-facing identity.
