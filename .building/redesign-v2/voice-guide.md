# Publishly voice guide (researched 2026-08-10: Buffer, Ayrshare, Metricool)

## The blend
- Buffer: benefit-led section titles (5-9 words, verb-led, conversational), warm
  second person, minimal jargon, short+medium sentence rhythm.
- Ayrshare: technical specificity AS credibility ("one call publishes to 13+
  networks", real endpoint names, concrete counts), low-friction CTAs.
- Publishly floor: every number real, every claim maps to shipped code, no
  invented proof (no user counts/testimonials until real).

## Rules
1. Titles are benefits with a mechanism behind them: "A week planned in one
   sitting" not "Powerful scheduling".
2. Bodies give the concrete how in ≤2 sentences: name the real thing (official
   OAuth, durable workflow, CSV preview, scoped keys, snapshots).
3. Second person, active voice, & over and, sentence case, numerals.
4. CTAs: "Get started free" / "See how it works" / "View API docs" + the
   reassurance line "No credit card needed. 7-day trial on every plan."
5. Credibility = specificity: "10 networks, one pipeline", "9 endpoints under
   /public/v1", "AGPL-3.0 — read the source". Never vanity numbers.
6. Ban: revolutionize, supercharge, seamlessly, unleash, dominate, game-changing,
   "the future of", unlimited reach, viral.
